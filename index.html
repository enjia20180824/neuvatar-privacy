Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2025  ·  Last updated: July 6, 2025

Neuvatar runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. No account is required. No analytics are embedded. Your data never leaves your phone.

1. Overview

Neuvatar ("the App") is a fully offline AI companion application developed by PLUS N Tech. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

Because all AI inference, voice processing, and data storage occur locally on your device, the scope of this policy is intentionally narrow: we collect nothing.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, process, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, device ID)
  • Conversation content or chat history
  • Voice recordings or audio data
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Payment or financial information

No data from your use of the App is ever sent to our servers or any third-party server.

3. On-Device Data Storage

All data generated by the App — including conversation history, persona configurations, voice profiles, and generated media — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage. This data is under your full control and is never accessible to PLUS N Tech or any external party.

Deleting the App permanently removes all locally stored data.

4. AI Model Files

On first launch, the App downloads AI model files (approximately 6–7 GB) from a public model hosting service. This download consists solely of model weights — no personal data is transmitted during or after this process. Once downloaded, the models operate fully offline.

5. Internet Permission

The App requests internet access solely for the one-time model file download described above. No other network activity occurs during normal use of the App.

6. Device Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Microphone — for voice input (processed locally, never transmitted)
  • Storage / Media — to save generated images and audio to your device
  • Camera — if used for avatar or image features (processed locally)

All permission usage is local and offline. You may revoke any permission at any time through your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because no data is collected from any user, there is no risk of unintentional collection from minors.

8. Third-Party Services

Neuvatar does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. There are no third-party services with access to your usage or data.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
